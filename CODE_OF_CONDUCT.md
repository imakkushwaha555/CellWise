# Community standards

Cellwise aims to be a welcoming, practical space for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and discuss ideas rather than attack people.
- Give useful, specific feedback and accept good-faith correction.
- Make room for questions and beginner contributions.
- Respect privacy: do not publish another person's private information, documents, or credentials.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and deliberately disruptive behavior.

Maintainers may edit or remove content that violates these standards and may restrict participation when necessary. If a concern arises, contact the repository maintainers privately through the reporting options listed in the repository. Those options are not configured until the GitHub repository exists.
